Drop your meme sound clips (.mp3/.wav/.m4a/.ogg) in this folder.

They'll be served publicly at:
  {PUBLIC_BASE_URL}/media/meme/<filename>

and played back in random order when a call with prank_type=meme_soundboard
is placed. Make sure files are short (a few seconds each) and that you have
the rights to use them -- e.g. royalty-free meme SFX packs, not ripped
copyrighted clips.
